# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, testing approach, and validation of acceptance criteria. They ensure features meet quality standards before release and identify defects early in the development cycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and test plans for features and releases
- Create and maintain test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual testing and coordinate automated test coverage
- Identify defects and work with developers to prioritize fixes
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Provide quality metrics and trending analysis

### Goals
- Ship high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Catch defects early to reduce production issues
- Build team confidence in the quality of releases

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews and test planning sessions
- Defect reports and triage conversations
- Weekly quality metrics in status updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to define testability requirements and validate implementations
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on QA timelines and release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, review technical designs, and help teams navigate complex technical decisions. They identify technical risks early and propose mitigation strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Review and provide feedback on technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Guide developers on best practices and code quality standards
- Participate in technical estimating and scope discussions
- Mentor junior team members on technical topics

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable system architecture
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team technical capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and mentoring
- Technical risk updates in planning and status meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guides **Developers** on technical direction and best practices
- Advises **Project Managers** and **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and risks
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** to design testable architectures

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They help teams work efficiently and adapt processes based on retrospective feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help identify and remove process blockers
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Manage sprint board and ensure transparency
- Track and follow up on retrospective action items

### Goals
- Enable smooth, predictable execution
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Remove friction from team workflows

### Typical Communication
- Leading agile ceremonies
- Follow-ups on action items from retrospectives
- Process improvement suggestions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports all team members in adopting agile practices
- Works with **Project Managers** to optimize delivery processes
- Coaches **Developers**, **Product Managers**, and other roles on collaboration and continuous improvement

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve project scope, and remove organizational blockers. They ensure projects align with business priorities and organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for projects
- Approve project scope, timeline, and budget
- Remove organizational and cross-functional blockers
- Communicate project value to leadership and other stakeholders
- Make decisions on trade-offs and priorities

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain organizational alignment
- Reduce organizational friction and blockers

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive briefings
- Decision gates on scope and priorities
- Escalation resolution and sponsor-level approvals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Project Managers** on scope, timeline, and risk escalation
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on business priorities and success metrics
- Provides strategic guidance to all team members

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience strategy, create design specifications, and validate usability. They ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs and organizational design standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and translate insights into design requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs through usability testing and user feedback
- Establish and maintain design system and standards
- Review feature implementations for design and usability compliance

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maintain consistent design and brand standards

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and interaction patterns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on user requirements and feature prioritization
- Works with **Developers** to ensure designs are implemented as specified
- Coordinates with **QA/Testing Leads** on usability testing and acceptance criteria

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and production readiness. They enable teams to deploy safely and efficiently while maintaining system reliability and performance.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code
- Automate build, test, and deployment processes
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Support production incidents and troubleshooting
- Ensure scalability, reliability, and disaster recovery planning

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments
- Maintain high system reliability and performance
- Reduce operational friction and manual work

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Infrastructure and performance metrics
- Incident response and post-mortems

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** on testing in CI/CD and deployment best practices
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on release planning and deployment windows
- Works with **QA/Testing Leads** to ensure testing is automated and efficient
- Advises **Technical Leads** on scalability and architectural concerns
