# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach to project delivery centered on customer value, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. Our processes support cross-functional collaboration and maintain transparency across all project phases.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

### Project Lifecycle Phases

The OctoAcme project lifecycle flows through five key phases, each with specific deliverables and checkpoints:

- **[Project Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md)** — Validate business need, align stakeholders, and create lightweight project charters. Includes the one-pager template and decision gate for moving into planning.

- **[Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md)** — Break work into shippable increments, estimate scope, identify dependencies, and establish timelines and milestones. Includes backlog item templates and Definition of Done guidance.

- **[Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)** — Manage day-to-day execution, track progress toward milestones, maintain team rhythm with standups and syncs, and escalate blockers. Includes quality and testing requirements, velocity tracking, and risk escalation paths.

- **[Release & Deployment](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)** — Standardize release processes, deployment checklists, pre-release verification, and rollback procedures. Includes release notes template and incident playbook.

- **[Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)** — Capture learnings after sprints or milestones, prioritize action items, and measure the impact of improvements over time.

### Supporting Disciplines

- **[Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)** — Identify, assess, track, and communicate risks and dependencies. Includes risk register structure, escalation paths, stakeholder communication templates, and incident communication guidance.

- **[Roles & Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)** — Define core project roles (Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA) and their responsibilities, goals, and typical communication patterns. Use these definitions to frame role-specific guidance and expectations.
